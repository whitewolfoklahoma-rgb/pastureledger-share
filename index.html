Help & support

New to PastureLedger, or stuck on something? Start with these short how-tos — they cover the jobs this app is used for every day. Tap a topic to open it.

Add livestock groups and keep counts true

Open the Livestock tab and fill in Add a group: pick the species, give the group a name, enter the head count and the paddock or field it is in, set the date in, then tap Add group. After that, use the buttons on each group card: Move group records a rotation to a new paddock, and Births / purchases / deaths / sales changes the head count — every move and count change is kept in the logs below the groups, where you can edit or delete an entry if you made a mistake.

Log money in and money out

Open the Money Log tab, set the date, choose Expense or Income, and pick a category — every category is already mapped to its IRS Schedule F line, which is shown next to it. Enter the amount and a short note about what it was, then tap Add entry. Don't enter sales here: sales logged in the Sales tab already count as income automatically, so entering them twice would double-count them.

Log a sale

Open the Sales tab and fill in Add a sale: the date, what you sold (eggs, whole chickens, beef, pork, live animals, and more), the quantity, the unit (dozen, each, lb, head…), and the price per unit, then tap Add sale. PastureLedger works out the total for you, and sales totals flow into the Dashboard and the Schedule F summary (Line 2, products you raised) on their own.

Read the Dashboard

The Dashboard tab shows the whole farm at a glance: total head and livestock groups, income, expenses, and net for this month and for the year, a month-by-month money table, and your most recent activity. Use the Tax year picker at the top of the screen to switch the year these figures — and the Schedule F summary — are shown for.

Get your Schedule F summary and exports

Open the Schedule F tab to see your Money Log and Sales totals grouped by IRS Schedule F line for the selected tax year, with gross income, total expenses, and net farm profit. Tap Print summary to print it for your tax preparer, or Download money log (CSV) / Download sales log (CSV) to save either log as a spreadsheet file. This is a planning summary to hand to your tax preparer — it is not a tax filing.

Enter records by voice

Most fields in PastureLedger have a 🎤 mic button beside them. Tap it once and speak — the words land in the field just as if you had typed them, and nothing is saved until you tap the usual save/add button. Numbers can be spoken naturally ("forty two head", "twelve hundred dollars"). Voice entry needs a browser/device that supports speech recognition (it works on the hosted and Google Play editions); where it isn't available, the mic shows a short note and typing works as always.

Back up your data and move it to a new device

Open the Settings tab. Under Backup, tap Download backup (JSON) to save a backup file of everything, and keep that file somewhere safe. On a new device or in a new browser, open the same place and use Import backup to bring your records back. Your farm name, owner, and tax year are also set in Settings.

Your data stays on this device

Your records live in this browser on this device (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Back them up regularly: in Settings, tap Download backup (JSON), and import that same file in Settings if you move to a new device or browser.
